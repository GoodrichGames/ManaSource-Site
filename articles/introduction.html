What is Mana Source?

  • Mana Source is an upcoming adventure board game for 1-4 players. You'll need to rely on your wits to survive. It features five playable primary classes, one exclusively secondary class, and over 240 unique action cards in the release set.


    Experiment, strategize, and mindgame to reduce your opponent's health to zero and win!
